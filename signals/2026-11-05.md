# Update 2026-11-05

*Window: 26 October–5 November 2026 · Verified against primary or reputable secondary sources; the figures now live in the research notes.*

## What changed

- 5 November: after midterm results favouring data-centre moratoria in Texas, Ohio and Georgia, the Nasdaq-100 fell 6.4% and the AI basket 12% in one session; the basket closed at 82.3 against 100 on 16 October ([public markets](/research/macro/public-markets), [power](/research/macro/power-and-datacenters)).
- 26 October and 2 November: [Anthropic](/research/stack/anthropic) filed its S-1 and S-1/A: Q3 revenue $17.0B, a GAAP operating loss of $567M, $131.8B of cash and $895.4B of commitments.
- 28-29 October: Azure grew 48%, Google Cloud 103% and AWS 39%, while free cash flow fell at [Microsoft](/research/stack/microsoft) and was negative at [Alphabet](/research/stack/alphabet) and, over twelve months, at [Amazon](/research/stack/amazon).
- 2-4 November: [Oracle](/research/stack/oracle) raised $10B of stock at $116 and $10B of notes at 7.0-8.65%, using its whole market-funding cap to FY2030 in a week.
- 26 October-5 November: [Meta](/research/stack/meta) sold $14.4B of preferred stock, $25B of shares forward and $15B of notes; S&P moved its outlook to negative.
- 5 November: [CoreWeave](/research/stack/coreweave)'s CDS was reported as high as 1,268bp and its 2032 notes at 74.75 cents; no payment was missed.
- The 2026 end-user revenue estimate fell from about $170B to about $160B once Anthropic's filed figures replaced leaks ([demand side](/research/macro/demand-side)).
- October OCPI means rose: H100 $2.94, B200 $8.50 ([GPU pricing](/research/macro/gpu-pricing-and-utilization)).
