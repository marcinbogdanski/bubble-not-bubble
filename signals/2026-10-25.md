## What changed since 21 October

- Meta released Q3 revenue of **$64.601bn**, capex including finance-lease principal of **$34.906bn**, and **−$2.120bn FCF**; strong advertising growth coexists with much heavier investment.
- Meta raised its 2026 capex floor **$130bn→$135bn**, keeping the ceiling at $145bn: midpoint **$137.5bn→$140bn, +1.82%**. This triggers **neither C1 nor C2**.
- Meta disclosed nonvoting Class C plans and September commitments: **$810.25bn** firm contracts plus uncommenced leases, or **$824.97bn** including up to $14.72bn contingent cloud commitments. These are not all debt or annual AI capex.
- Tesla lowered its open-ended 2026 capex threshold from **over $25bn to over $20bn**; its newly filed SpaceX committee confirms exploration already known on 21 October, not agreed merger terms.
- Lam reported **$8.219bn** quarterly revenue and **35% China exposure**, and forecasts preliminary **2027 WFE above $200bn**; the latter is management's outlook, not orders.
- Credit stress persisted and broadened: Meta CDS reached **114.79bp Markit / ~119.3bp ICE intraday** on 22 October. Bank access nevertheless remained open selectively: **Nebius $3.5bn** and **TeraWulf ~$150m added committed capacity**.
- OCPI settlements on 24 October were **H100 $3.02 / B200 $9.57 per GPU-hour**; Silicon Data's dated H100 card/chart showed **$2.88**, with a stale conflicting FAQ. No verified spot-price collapse.
- OpenAI's **up-to-$30bn at $1.25tn pre-money** is revised funding talk, not a closed down round; Anthropic's November IPO remains reported plans and its leaked accounts largely repeat the baseline.
- China's listed-model rout deepened, but MiniMax rebounded **16.74% on 23 October**; its below-IPO observation belongs to 22 October. Suffolk approved a temporary datacenter ban; Loudoun's proposed pause remains deferred.
- Microsoft/Alphabet **28 October**, Amazon **29 October**, FOMC **27–28 October**, Jupiter motions **26 October**, and CoreWeave's cure-rule transition **28 October** remain future events.

## How to read the verification

Numbered rows consolidate the broad scan (**G1–G36**) and company pass (**C1–C18**); repeated leads are handled once. **Confirms** means the cited source supports the material claim; **partly** identifies a corrected date, scope, definition, unsupported precision or incomplete fetch; **does not confirm** means the asserted fact was not established, not that access failure proves it false. Grades follow [methodology](/report/methodology): A directly stated primary; B reputable reporting or simple primary-source arithmetic; C leak, estimate or individual reported market snapshot; D tertiary, contradicted or unreproduced detail. A management forecast receives A for its publication, not its eventual accuracy. Private-company leaks are **reported, unverified, C** even when the existence of the report is confirmed.

**New** is measured against the 21 October **15:00 UTC information set**, not the start of that calendar day. **Yes** = later verified observation/disclosure; **No** = older/recirculated; **Mixed** = new disclosure/reporting containing older underlying events; **Unestablished** = no independently verified new observation. Old facts newly retrieved are labelled. Quotes are snapshots, not synchronized closing marks. Research-note changes below are instructions for later upkeep; this entry does not edit those notes or the forecast register.
