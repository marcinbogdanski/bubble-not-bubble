# Signals: 2026-10-17 (week of 10-17 October)

*Codex verification. Sources accessed/attempted 2026-10-17; failures identified. Currency: USD.*

## What changed this week

- CoreWeave credit protection reached a reported intraday record of **1,194.5bp**; its unsecured debt traded at distressed yields (item 1).
- Oracle protection also widened, while project debt sold off; individual quotes are snapshots, not closing-price series (items 2–3).
- Benchmark rates did **not** rise throughout the week: the Treasury ten-year par yield fell from **5.24% to 5.09%** (item 31).
- Anthropic reportedly generated **$16.8bn** in quarterly revenue and another adjusted profit; accounts remain unavailable publicly (item 14).
- OpenAI's **approximately $50bn** September annualisation uses different partner accounting; its report predates this window (item 15).
- Blackstone was reportedly offered **more than 10%** on proposed Anthropic chip financing; a completed transaction is unverified (item 10).
- GPU indexes still show paying demand, but measure different markets and contain conflicting vintages (items 22–25).
- China proposed technology-export controls and began interim registration; hosted model access has important exemptions (item 29).
- Older stories recur; corrections concern financing, PPI and an existing SpaceX contract.
