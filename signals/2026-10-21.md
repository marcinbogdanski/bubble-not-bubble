## Reading this verification

The **61 numbered bundles** below cover both concatenated scans, consolidating overlaps. **G** denotes the broad scan's item number; **C** denotes the company pass's material-item number. The company pass's calendar and outside-window context are reconciled separately. Counts refer to bundles, not independent facts or sources.

**Confirmed** means the material rendering was traced; **partly** means an important component, period, definition or figure needs correction or remains unchecked; **unconfirmed** means the substantive numerical/absence claim was not established (including a contradicted claim); **lead only** means an unverified attributed opinion. A failed fetch does not prove a claim false. Private-company leaks remain **reported, unverified, C**, even when the publication of the report is confirmed. A primary company's forecast or index display earns A for what was published, not for the future outcome or market representativeness.

Novelty is measured against the **17 October information set**, with the [17 October log](/signals/2026-10-17) and [18 October verification](/signals/2026-10-18-market-views) as comparators: **Yes** = verified new observation/disclosure after 17 October; **No** = older/recirculated; **Mixed** = fresh reporting alongside older underlying figures; **Unestablished** = no verified new observation. Old facts newly found in this verification are labelled explicitly. These categories prevent a fresh article date from turning an old contract or permit order into a new event.
