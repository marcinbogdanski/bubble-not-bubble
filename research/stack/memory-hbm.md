# Memory and HBM: SK hynix, Samsung Memory and Micron

*Last updated: 2026-10-17 · Status: draft · Author: Codex / GPT-6 · Reviewer: none*

## Summary

- Memory is comfortably profitable today. Micron's latest GAAP gross/operating margins are 86.8%/80.7%; SK hynix reports a 76% operating margin. These are scarcity earnings across conventional memory as well as HBM, not evidence of permanently higher through-cycle returns. [M4][H2]
- Micron has reported fiscal Q4, Samsung has issued consolidated Q3 guidance, and SK hynix's latest located results are Q2. Samsung's Q3 guidance contains no Memory segment result. [M4][S3][HI]
- Longer agreements shift some expansion risk to customers. Uncontracted output, execution and renewal prices remain exposed. [MR]
- HBM leadership is contestable: Counterpoint estimates Q2 revenue shares of 50% SK hynix, 33% Samsung and 18% Micron, subject to rounding. Shipment share, revenue share and a company's HBM revenue mix are different measures. [CP]
- Nvidia's larger supply commitments, lower margin outlook, Amazon's higher capex and equipment suppliers' memory growth are consistent with the same shortage. They are linked observations, not independent end-demand confirmations. [NC][NT][AC][AT]
- The expansion calendar is staggered. Some meaningful additions start in 2027, but important Micron and SK hynix projects arrive later. Simultaneous yield improvement and customer retrenchment could still turn the market before every new fab opens. [MD][HN]
- Cash generation, public equity and subsidies support expansion. Inadequate returns after prices normalize are the main vulnerability. Contractual protection through a demand shock remains uncertain. [M4][H2][CITI][S2]

## Analysis

Data: [Micron](/data/memory-micron-quarterly.csv), [SK hynix](/data/memory-skhynix-quarterly.csv), [Samsung](/data/memory-samsung-quarterly.csv), [HBM revenue shares](/data/memory-hbm-market-share-quarterly.csv). Blanks mean unavailable or uncollected; Samsung preliminary guidance is explicitly separated from actuals.

**What is earning the money.** HBM serves accelerators, conventional DRAM supplies system memory and NAND persistent storage. Shared AI demand meets different manufacturing constraints. Micron's margins establish current profitability; Cloud Memory includes other products. Samsung reports Memory sales but broader DS profits. Neither permits a clean comparison of HBM-only margins. [M4][S2]

Use SK hynix's operating profit rather than net profit distorted by nonoperating gains. Its estimated HBM mix can fall while HBM shipments grow if conventional-memory prices rise faster; the absolute HBM estimate remains tentative. [SP][HG]

**HBM changes the cycle's transmission.** Customization and qualification impede substitution. SK hynix links supply to Nvidia's roadmap; Samsung identifies HBM4 sales for Vera Rubin. Micron previously confirmed price/volume agreements for its entire calendar-2026 HBM supply; Samsung said its production-ready capacity was sold out. These statements concern planned capacity, not unlimited future supply. Micron's latest update contracts most next-year bits; Samsung confirms next-year agreements without specifying complete coverage. [HP][S1][MT1][ST1][MD][ST]

Micron files binding volumes, take-or-pay terms and deposit retention rights. Deposits secure capacity and enter financing cash flow, outside operating FCF. [MK] Our inference: they improve liquidity and enforcement, but repayment may coincide with weaker demand; protection depends on counterparty credit and recovery.

**Pricing power cuts across products.** TrendForce's contract forecast still points upward, while its wafer-economics work says conventional server memory became more attractive than annually priced HBM. Micron likewise says next-year HBM price increases narrow its margin gap with conventional DRAM. HBM is therefore not automatically the industry's highest-margin product at every moment. Annual pricing can initially restrain upside and later defer downside. [TP][TW][MR]

Nvidia's final HBM4 prices and terms are undisclosed. TrendForce's blended ASP forecast includes generation and stack mix, rather than a uniform like-for-like price increase. Its reported consideration of less memory per device shows buyers responding to cost. [TH]

**Reconciling the adjacent notes.** Nvidia's commitment increase buys multi-year supply assurance; it should not be booked as memory manufacturers' current revenue or read as wholly HBM. Its margin guidance places some scarcity cost at the accelerator vendor before price increases flow through. Amazon places another portion in buyers' capital budgets. ASML's memory growth and SEMI's DRAM equipment forecast describe the supplier response, including process transitions rather than solely additional wafer starts. [NC][NT][AC][AT][SE]

Inference: nominal capex can rise without more physical capacity; forward purchases can obscure final demand elasticity. Linked commitments do not independently verify end-user returns.

**When supply actually arrives.** M15X and Yongin's first phase are the near-term SK hynix additions. Its newly approved Yongin Y2 and Cheongju M17 have later cleanroom dates. Micron's schedule separates Idaho and Taiwan wafer output, Singapore packaging, later Japan and Singapore NAND output, and a still later New York start. First wafers require ramping; packaging adds no DRAM wafer capacity. Samsung confirms Pyeongtaek investment but the reviewed disclosures do not establish a precise fab-by-fab qualified-output calendar. [H2][HN][MD][ST]

Overlapping capacity ramps, yield improvements and weaker demand could reverse prices without every fab opening together. Investment returns require paid demand for each vintage and disciplined equipment installation. Construction schedules alone establish neither a glut nor its absence. [HN][MD]

**Financing and loss bearers.** Micron's current operating cash generation covers gross physical investment before incentives; customer security and public subsidies add protection. SK hynix has substantial net cash and also accessed public equity through the ADS offering. Samsung's consolidated liquidity and OCF comfortably exceed disclosed cash PP&E spending, although neither is attributable entirely to Memory. Management capex and cash purchases have different recognition bases and must remain separate series. [M4][H2][CITI][S2][ST]

Samsung Memory draws on Samsung Electronics' consolidated balance sheet. SK hynix is a listed issuer within SK, with investment holding company SK Square a shareholder. Parent investment income is not additional subsidiary cash; affiliation establishes no blanket creditor guarantee. [SQ] Our loss-allocation inference: equity absorbs impaired returns first, contracted customers may lose deposits, and lenders retain residual default risk.

**History remains relevant.** Micron's reversal from high gross profitability to negative gross margin, and SK hynix's annual operating loss, show that technical leadership does not eliminate fixed-cost exposure. Historical server contract-price falls were fast enough to meet a P2-sized decline within a quarter for some products. LTAs may protect selected revenue while uncontracted conventional memory sets the marginal market price. HBM can dampen or delay the cycle and transfer losses to customers; it cannot make unwanted capacity economically productive. [MH][HH][T22][T23]

**Proposed P2 definition.** Use the fixed TrendForce **DDR5 8GB SO-DIMM contract-price Session Average**, in dollars per module, at the final assessment in each calendar month. Define the baseline as the maximum of those observations during calendar 2026. Resolve yes if any later monthly observation published by the relevant register horizon is at or below the threshold in claim 39. Freeze product, currency and contract basis; do not substitute the site's more recent spot table. A single observation preserves the register's “at any point” meaning without introducing a persistence requirement. [PX]

This fixed-product proxy excludes server DRAM and HBM. The public contract table lags newer spot data and a member-only contract update. The unverified baseline makes the example conditional. Archive assessments; adjudicate discontinuation explicitly. The register and its probabilities are unchanged. [PX]

## Conflicts and open questions

- **Protected versus cyclical:** Micron warns of contractual disputes and delivery liability. Floors and credit quality determine protection. [MK]
- **A synchronized supply glut versus continued shortages:** supplier schedules show staggered additions and staged equipment installation. Samsung expects tight supply through 2028 and TrendForce forecasts next-year HBM scarcity. Those forecasts oppose an imminent-glut thesis, but suppliers and customers can share an optimistic demand error. [ST][HN][TH]
- **Market-share disagreement:** IDC and Counterpoint differ in claim 27. Neither estimate is audited company revenue; comparisons require consistent periods and denominators. [HF][CP]
- **Backlog date trap:** claim 8 compares year-end RPO with a later call update. No reconciliation was verified. RPO excludes certain variable-price arrangements and is not all expected sales. [MK][MR]
- **Unverified scope:** current HBM revenue and total-revenue mix for Micron and Samsung; company-confirmed SK hynix HBM dollars; a universal industry sellout through 2027; Nvidia's supplier-specific prices and cancellation rights; precise Samsung fab output dates; numerical 2027 capex budgets for both Korean vendors. Original contracts, segment disclosures and qualified-output data would resolve these gaps.
