# The non-Nvidia accelerator layer: AMD, Broadcom, Marvell, hyperscaler silicon, Intel, Cerebras/Groq

*Last updated: 2026-11-05 · Cross-checked by: Codex gpt-6-astra · Data: [stack-amd-data-center-quarterly.csv](/data/stack-amd-data-center-quarterly.csv), [stack-broadcom-ai-revenue-quarterly.csv](/data/stack-broadcom-ai-revenue-quarterly.csv), [stack-marvell-data-center-quarterly.csv](/data/stack-marvell-data-center-quarterly.csv)*

## Summary

- AMD supplies merchant GPUs and CPUs; Broadcom and Marvell supply custom silicon and connectivity. AMD's $8.546B Data Center revenue includes CPUs and MI450 launch sales; Broadcom's $16.7B AI semiconductor revenue includes networking. Neither isolates accelerator economics (claims 1, 11).
- AMD is profitable and liquid, but Q3 FCF is $554M on $13.377B revenue. Its $15.776B liquidity accompanies $7.079B debt, $48.761B operating purchase commitments and separate partner-support exposures (claims 2–3, 7–9, 27, 29).
- AMD customer warrants now affect earnings despite no vesting: a ~$1.2B noncash charge and $4.5B unrecognized grant-date value to reduce qualifying revenue. Initial Helios racks are installed at Meta; this does not establish full deployment (claims 5–6).
- Broadcom's strong consolidated cash generation coexists with customer credit risk. A committed $63B Anthropic SPV financing structure carries a contingent ~$43B residual-value backstop. S&P affirms A− with a stable outlook (claims 12, 17, 24, 30).
- Direct demand remains concentrated. Broadcom has six XPU customers; only OpenAI's initial 1 GW is explicitly binding in AMD's cited agreement. AMD also funds customer equity, reinforcing the link between supplier sales and customer finance (claims 4, 13, 28).
- Custom silicon may lower workload-specific costs, but modeled TCO savings do not establish useful-token returns. Marvell is GAAP-profitable; Cerebras reports losses. The biggest uncertainty remains customers' ability to service deployed capacity and suppliers' contingent obligations (claims 19, 21–23; [Anthropic](/research/stack/anthropic)).

## Analysis

AMD's MI450 launch and EPYC demand support growing Data Center sales, and Q4 guidance anticipates further expansion. Meta's installed test racks are operational evidence, not proof that gigawatt targets have been delivered. Neither AMD's segment nor Broadcom's networking-inclusive AI category isolates accelerator demand or margins (claims 1, 5, 11, 26). Enterprises can buy through labs and clouds despite concentrated direct buyers.

AMD's cash conversion is less comfortable than its revenue growth: FCF is $554M, or 4.14% of sales. Liquidity is substantial, but the balance sheet also includes $7.079B debt after bond issuance and a maturity repayment (claims 2–3, 7). Operating purchases of $48.761B are distinct from $6.175B partner cloud-capacity commitments, which decline as third parties pay (claims 8, 27). Partner leases intended for assignment or sublease and guarantees leave AMD exposed if customers cannot fund deployments. The $750M debt-guarantee component has a short remaining term; no partner default or guarantee payment was reported (claims 9, 29). These are differently timed obligations, not an immediate cash bill or additive expected loss.

AMD's warrant accounting now specifies two channels: grant-date consideration reduces qualifying revenue, while subsequent liability remeasurement affects other income or expense until exercisability. The ~$1.2B quarterly noncash charge and $4.5B unrecognized grant-date value demonstrate economic effects before any share vests (claim 6). They are neither a current cash payment nor a universal GPU discount. AMD's announced Meta equity purchase and its filed unnamed October investment are consistent, but the filing alone does not identify the recipient or establish when Meta receives proceeds (claim 28; [Meta financing](/research/stack/meta)).

Broadcom combines strong cash generation with retained credit exposure. Its consolidated FCF and margins include software; RPO spans semiconductors and software and has termination provisions (claims 12, 14–15). The $63B SPV structure funds purchases for lease to Anthropic, with first-lien bank financing and committed second-lien support from Blackstone. Broadcom retains a deployment-dependent residual-value backstop, capped at approximately $43B and reduced by lease payments and recoveries (claims 17, 24). The announcement establishes committed financing, not full funding or deliveries. Management's belief that the backstop is unlikely to be called is not an independently measured default probability.

The conditional $42B note facility remains an August quarter-end disclosure; the new SPV documents do not reconcile its overlap with that facility or other support. Do not sum these figures into a funded exposure. Recovery rights matter, but correlated customer weakness and falling rack values could impair both collections and collateral. The stable S&P outlook reflects a less favorable credit assessment without a downgrade (claims 18, 30).

Custom silicon can change who captures margin, but chip-hour TCO estimates are not measured useful-token economics (claim 19). Google's external TPU system sales contribute to Cloud growth; their scale, profitability and repurchase provisions belong in the [Alphabet note](/research/stack/alphabet). Marvell is profitable; Cerebras has losses and substantial liquidity; Groq's announcement establishes continued independent operations (claims 21–23, 25). Anthropic's public filing distinguishes GAAP operating losses from adjusted profit and discloses contractual maturities; its financial figures belong in the [Anthropic note](/research/stack/anthropic). Customer cash generation, unrestricted funding and payment schedules remain the test of supplier collectability.

## Open questions

- How much of AMD's revenue is warrant-adjusted, and how will purchase milestones and share prices affect future revenue reductions and remeasurement charges (claim 6)?
- Which Broadcom facilities and lease backstops overlap? The SPV announcement and 8-K do not bridge all outstanding support, draws and payment schedules (claims 17–18, 24).
- Can AMD assign partner leases and place reserved cloud capacity without retaining losses? What finances repayment of short-term guaranteed partner debt (claims 9, 27, 29)?
- Broadcom semiconductor-margin transcripts still conflict; company IR or call audio is needed (claim 15). Marvell's alleged Trainium design loss remains reported, unverified (claim 20).
- AMD-led bookings at Samsung's Taylor expansion remain anonymous-source claims: grade C, reported, unverified, not confirmed AMD supply ([TrendForce](https://www.trendforce.com/news/2026/11/04/news-samsungs-2nm-taylor-fab-2-reportedly-fully-booked-before-2030-output-with-amd-leading-hbm-base-die-orders/)).
- Do deployed systems earn returns after porting, utilization, financing and residual-value costs? Vendor outlooks do not establish customer returns. Named completion evidence for AMD's Meta investment and Meta's proceeds timing remains incomplete (claim 28).
