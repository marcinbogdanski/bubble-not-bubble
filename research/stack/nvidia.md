# Nvidia

*Last updated: 2026-11-05 · Cross-checked by: Claude claude-fable-5-1, Codex gpt-6-astra · Data: [nvidia-quarterly.csv](/data/nvidia-quarterly.csv)*

## Summary

- Nvidia supplies AI compute and is comfortably profitable. Quarterly revenue is $96.221B, gross margin 75.0%, and trailing FCF $126.9B (claims 1, 3, 5).
- Customer support ties its balance sheet to demand. Supply commitments are $279B, equity investments carry at $99B, and the conditional Ohio guarantee is capped at $105B. A $3B Meta equity investment is announced, not established as funded; separate Nscale guarantee talks remain reported, unverified (claims 11, 13–14, 26, 29).
- Receivables are $63.059B with DSO of 60 days. Extended terms support customers, but do not alone prove collection problems (claim 9).
- Supported labs are expected to supply roughly a quarter of FY2028 business. Their current loss-funded share is unquantified. Rubin availability on OCI supports deployment progress, but planned expansion is not a quantified binding order (claims 8, 15, 27).
- Gross margin is guided lower, with recovery dependent on price increases. Guidance assumes no China Data Center compute. Reuters reports, unverified, about 600,000 H200s awaiting mainland import approval in Hong Kong; this does not establish global excess supply (claims 3, 6, 30).
- Strong cash generation, AA/stable credit and discretionary buybacks argue against imminent distress. The rating predates Ohio; the reported five-year CDS snapshot is 128bp, a market risk price rather than a default finding (claims 5, 21–22, 25).
- Valuation requires sustained future growth and margins. The biggest uncertainty is whether customer funding and project delivery can sustain demand as Nvidia's procurement and financing exposure grow. Q3 results are pending on 17 November (claims 6, 11–19, 24, 26, 29).

## Analysis

Nvidia's earnings and cash generation are strong, but customer overbuild can still impair future orders, margins and investment values. GAAP earnings include equity gains; adjusted net income removes those gains with associated tax adjustments. Pretax gains cannot simply be subtracted from after-tax earnings (claim 4). A comparable adjusted trailing earnings multiple remains unavailable because the reported series changes its treatment of stock-based compensation.

The financing exposure runs through distinct instruments. Receivables defer customer cash payments; equity absorbs investment losses; capacity agreements commit Nvidia to cloud purchases; guarantees cover contractual shortfalls after protections and recoveries. These cannot be summed as immediate losses. Customer advances run in the opposite direction, while equity lock-ups restrict liquidity (claims 9, 12–14, 17–20, 23).

The Ohio cap becomes effective only with readiness conditions. Recovery offsets, reimbursement and termination rights limit exposure, but do not ensure recovery during a correlated AI downturn. Compute's ability to serve other users does not establish that a particular site retains its financing value. SB Energy's unfinished gas-generation arrangements add execution risk. The Nscale convertible-note commitment is distinct from reported guarantee discussions, which establish no additional contractual exposure. The Meta equity announcement extends supplier support to a profitable hyperscaler; neither investment completion nor immediate cash receipt by Meta follows from it (claims 13, 18–19, 26, 29).

Supply commitments create a separate risk if demand slips. Limited disclosed RPO cannot establish a matching firm chip backlog. Inventory can rise during a product ramp, but procurement and inventory become more dangerous if delivery or funding falters (claims 10–11, 23). DSO reflects extended terms to certain investment-grade buyers; early customer payments affected the preceding comparison. Aging and cash conversion are needed to distinguish normalization from deterioration (claim 9).

Direct customers include distribution and integration channels. Their shares do not identify ultimate users or how purchases are funded. The unnamed indirect lab may be OpenAI, but the filing does not identify it. Hyperscale and ACIE are business classifications, not funding categories. The expected supported-lab share concerns future total-company business, not today's loss-funded demand (claims 2, 8, 15). The [circular-financing note](/research/macro/circular-financing) owns the accounting-scale scenarios; they do not establish that dependence.

[Meta's funding position](/research/stack/meta) illustrates buyer cash pressure. [GPU rental readings](/research/macro/gpu-pricing-and-utilization) counter an immediate rental-price collapse but do not measure utilization. OCI's general availability is concrete deployment evidence; its expansion plans do not establish funded backlog or realized sales (claim 27).

Input costs and product mix pressure margins, while competition remains an additional risk. Management's recovery path assumes realized price increases; disclosures cannot isolate each cause. China-related headquarters revenue measures something different from the absence of China compute in guidance. The reported H200 stockpile concerns import restrictions, not demonstrated global surplus; without ownership and recognition data it cannot be added to Nvidia inventory or receivables (claims 3, 6, 28, 30).

The dated valuation multiples require sustained growth and margins. GAAP earnings include investment gains, and provider and derived multiples differ in basis. A price multiple alone does not establish cheapness or fair value (claim 24).

Equity repricing can precede defaults. Guarantees can protect lenders while moving losses to Nvidia. Contractual seniority, collateral, triggers and recoveries decide ultimate losses. Current FCF, discretionary repurchases and the credit assessment provide counterevidence to near-term insolvency, while correlated customer funding remains the central risk. The reported CDS quote measures priced risk, not a rating action or distressed event (claims 5, 13, 21–22, 25).

## Open questions

- What share of demand depends on supplier support or loss-making labs? Customer-level cash allocation and ultimate-user data are missing.
- How do funded investments reconcile with announcements? OpenAI's paid-in total, Meta completion, overlap among arrangements and investment reclassifications remain unverified. Do Nscale guarantee talks become signed terms, and with what cap, triggers and recoveries?
- Can Ohio be delivered and relet at values that protect Nvidia during a downturn? Project financing, readiness evidence and recovery terms matter.
- Will receivable aging and cash conversion support management's explanation of longer terms?
- How much margin pressure comes from memory costs, mix or competition? Realized pricing and product-level economics would help.
- Who owns the reported Hong Kong H200 stock, and when is revenue recognized? Import approvals and inventory disclosures must distinguish channel blockage from unsold Nvidia inventory.
- Rental trackers use different vendors and contracts. Comparable rents, utilization and realized collateral recoveries are still needed.
