## Analysis

**Demand and financing.** China expands infrastructure demand, but group capex includes general cloud, CPUs and facilities. Alibaba's fiscal year ends in March; Tencent and Baidu use calendar years. Private-company budgets are prospective. Adding these figures into “China AI capex” would manufacture comparability.

Alibaba's multi-year commitment is a floor, with an announced but unquantified increase. Actual spending and its capacity target support acceleration. Component inflation and procurement timing mean expenditure need not translate proportionately into useful compute. Revenue growth is encouraging; negative FCF and the equity placement show shareholders funding expansion and bearing dilution and impairment risk.[1](https://www.alibabagroup.com/en-US/document-1830678592242057216)–[5](https://www.alibabagroup.com/en-US/document-2029365886510432256), [50](https://home.alibabagroup.com/en-US/document-2041268239081668608)

In the June 2026 quarter, Alibaba reported adjusted EBITA of +RMB5.628bn in AI Cloud and Compute Services and −RMB13.861bn in AI Labs and Applications. These segment results distinguish profitable infrastructure services from model/application investment losses; the cloud segment also includes non-AI services. Standalone inference margins and investment-cohort returns remain undisclosed.[51](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1577552/000110465926099220/tm2623667d1_ex99-1.htm)

Alibaba's operating cash less group capex was −RMB44.733bn, distinct from reported FCF of −RMB44.670bn: its FCF bridge uses RMB67.660bn of eligible purchases and a +RMB0.045bn buyer-protection-deposit adjustment (claim 46). This is a definition difference, not an arithmetic error.[51](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1577552/000110465926099220/tm2623667d1_ex99-1.htm)

Tencent remains profitable. Its Q2 2026 FCF was −RMB13.8bn, but management reports +RMB37.6bn excluding compute prepayments. Procurement timing explains the sign reversal; cash commitments and delivery/utilization risks remain. The FCF reconciliation subtracts cash capex, media content and lease payments; prepayments already depress operating cash and must not be subtracted again (claims 44–45). The offering extract's RMB96.3bn half-year cash payments and interim report's RMB84.720bn reported capex are distinct measures.[8](https://www.tencent.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Tencent-Announces-2026-Second-Quarter-Results.pdf), [62](https://www.tencent.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/E700_Launch-Announcement.pdf), [7](https://www.tencent.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/E700_IR.pdf) Management also describes a more favourable cloud pricing environment, evidence against a universal price war. Baidu similarly invests beyond current operating cash; its investment holdings provide liquidity, although “cash and investments” exceeds immediately available cash. These cash shortfalls concern the June quarter's consolidated groups, not normalized annual or AI-only operations.[8](https://www.tencent.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Tencent-Announces-2026-Second-Quarter-Results.pdf), [10](https://ir.baidu.com/news-releases/news-release-details/baidu-announces-second-quarter-2026-results)

ByteDance's reported 2025 budget is contested: Reuters reports the figure and ByteDance's explicit rejection. Its May 2026 annual spending scenario and profit estimate remain reported, unverified, and cannot be directly compared with Alibaba's multiyear floor.[11](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/exclusivebytedance-plans-20-billion-capex-in-2025-mostly-on-ai-sources-say-3826261?ampMode=1), [12](https://theedgemalaysia.com/node/805120)

Financing already includes debt channels. On October 14, 2026, Tencent announced pricing of US$1.8bn and CNY26bn of notes for general corporate purposes, with issuance expected October 21; this does not allocate every dollar to AI.[52](https://www.tencent.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/E700_Pricing-Announcement.pdf) Reuters reported a US$29.6bn unsecured ByteDance facility on September 4, with an initial three-year term and extension options; general corporate purposes were communicated to lenders, while AI/offshore allocations came from anonymous sources. This is reported, unverified, not a verified drawdown.[53](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/bytedance-secures-296-billion-loan-in-ai-push-sources-say-4889439) On October 6, Reuters reported a prospective DeepSeek round exceeding RMB80bn, expected to close in October; closing is not established by the article.[54](https://www.investing.com/news/economy-news/deepseek-set-to-net-over-12-billion-in-new-fundraising-source-says-4933887) These reports broaden the potential loss bearers beyond profit-funded expansion without establishing complete financing schedules.

Private labs depend on investors and infrastructure partners; government funding also supports semiconductor manufacturers.[25](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/chinas-memory-chip-makers-ride-ai-boom-to-new-power--and-us-scrutiny-4810472) Shareholders, strategic backers and asset owners bear dilution and impairment risk. Announced Tencent notes and ByteDance's reported unsecured facility make lender exposure concrete as a financing channel, subject to issuance/drawdown; landlords and public funders also bear risk where contracts or guarantees create exposure. The loss allocation remains incompletely disclosed.[52](https://www.tencent.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/E700_Pricing-Announcement.pdf), [53](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/bytedance-secures-296-billion-loan-in-ai-push-sources-say-4889439)

**DeepSeek and domestic supply.** V3's arithmetic measures an official run at assumed rental prices, excluding earlier experiments. It is neither hardware acquisition cost nor the cost of discovering the recipe.[13](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.19437v2) The later Nature supplement separately estimates specified R1 stages on top of an inherited base. Neither figure establishes a complete development budget or serving margin.[14](https://media.springernature.com/original/springer-static/esm/art%3A10.1038%2Fs41586-025-09422-z/MediaObjects/41586_2025_9422_MOESM1_ESM.pdf)

DeepSeek's current Flash model is V4.1-Flash-1017, updated October 17, 2026 with API pricing unchanged; the April V4 preview is historical. A separate team's paper demonstrates V4-family post-training on Ascend, without establishing that original frontier pretraining ran entirely on Huawei.[15](https://deepseek.com/en/news/v4-preview/), [16](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20145), [58](https://api-docs.deepseek.com/updates/) On September 30, DeepSeek released an Ascend backend in TileKernels and sparse-attention inference kernels in FlashMLA. These are concrete software adaptations, stronger evidence than a roadmap, but do not verify full-system cost parity, reliability or shipped capacity.[55](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/TileKernels), [56](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/FlashMLA/blob/main/docs/20260930-ascend-prefill-deep-dive.md) Bloomberg's later site report explicitly concerns inference; the proposed accelerators fill only part of the site, without establishing installed capacity or utilization.[17](https://www.bloomberglinea.com/negocios/deepseek-planea-instalar-160000-chips-huawei-en-un-megacentro-para-reducir-su-dependencia-de-nvidia/)

Domestic substitution extends beyond Ascend. Alibaba introduced Zhenwu V900 on September 22, 2026, advertising 3× predecessor performance and clusters up to 500,000 cards. Huawei's September 19 ecosystem announcement offers shared 10k-NPU-scale compute access and programming support. These vendor disclosures widen the supply and software pathways; accepted shipments, actual utilization, qualified HBM volume and comparable cost per successful task remain unverified.[60](https://home.alibabagroup.com/en-US/document-2039431633571938304), [61](https://www.huawei.com/en/news/2026/9/hc-agentic-thinkpro-pto-cann)

Huawei's roadmap cannot settle manufacturing yield, qualified HBM supply or cluster economics.[18](https://www.huawei.com/en/news/2025/9/hc-xu-keynote-speech) Component-chip production estimates are not finished-accelerator counts. HBM controls and reported scarcity-driven processor price increases contradict unlimited cheap domestic compute.[17](https://www.bloomberglinea.com/negocios/deepseek-planea-instalar-160000-chips-huawei-en-un-megacentro-para-reducir-su-dependencia-de-nvidia/)–[20](https://www.bis.gov/press-release/commerce-strengthens-export-controls-restrict-chinas-capability-produce-advanced-semiconductors-military)

SMIC's utilization and positive gross margin show manufacturing demand, without disclosing advanced-node good-die output or Ascend profitability. Original teardown evidence supports progress while contradicting leading-node parity.[21](https://smic.cdn.shwebspace.com/uploads/6a7d7c4f/ER_EN.pdf), [22](https://www.techinsights.com/ja/node/60833) CXMT DRAM and YMTC NAND widen domestic options; NAND cannot substitute for accelerator HBM, and a DRAM process announcement does not establish packaging yield or HBM qualification.[23](https://www.cxmt.com/news/info_97.html), [24](https://www.ymtc.com/en/technicalintroduction.html) Substitution can reduce import dependence while increasing resource cost per useful token.

**Prices and inference margins.** Open weights enable competing hosts and credible migration threats; cheap APIs can pressure scarcity pricing. The tariff samples establish offers, not a quality-adjusted price series or a causal estimate of global deflation. Not every hosted variant in an open-weight family has released weights.[26](https://www.alibabacloud.com/help/en/model-studio/model-pricing)–[29](https://www.minimax.io/blog/minimax-m25)

Western margins collapse only if substitution works at comparable task success, latency, reliability and governance, and price reductions outrun cost reductions and usage growth. Cheaper tokens can expand agent usage and equipment demand while destroying model rents. Kimi's premium tier and Tencent's pricing commentary contradict universal commoditization.[8](https://www.tencent.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Tencent-Announces-2026-Second-Quarter-Results.pdf), [27](https://platform.kimi.com/docs/pricing/chat) Standalone inference margins after depreciation, power and unsuccessful outputs, and returns by investment cohort, remain undisclosed; Alibaba's segment EBITA is observable but does not resolve them.[51](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1577552/000110465926099220/tm2623667d1_ex99-1.htm)

DeepSeek's September 10 V4.1-Flash launch claims KV-cache HBM demand at one-quarter of the predecessor's, rather than a reduction of all memory needs. Its October 17 update claims shorter runtimes and outputs with unchanged API pricing. Lower task costs can therefore reflect efficiency alongside competitive rent pressure; these vendor claims do not establish realized returns.[57](https://www.deepseek.com/en/news/deepseek-v4-1-flash/), [58](https://api-docs.deepseek.com/updates/)

**Permission from both governments.** H20 illustrates stranded inventory risk. A disclosed revenue-share expectation does not establish a statutory tax or collections. H200 subsequently operates under a distinct combination of conditional US licensing, Chinese restrictions and a separate tariff.[30](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581025000209/nvda-20250727.htm)–[33](https://media.bis.gov/press-release/department-commerce-revises-license-review-policy-semiconductors-exported-china) Blackwell variants and tracking proposals do not establish a broad approved data-centre channel.[34](https://www.tradingview.com/news/reuters.com%2C2025%3Anewsml_L2N3V202J%3A0-nvidia-s-new-rtx6000d-chip-for-china-finds-little-favour-with-major-firms-sources-say/), [35](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/house-bill/3447)

MOFCOM combines a draft catalogue revision with already-effective temporary pre-export registration, including started but unfinished exports and specified foreign ownership/control changes. Under the proposed final regime, public-domain/open-source exclusions and API/app/SaaS service exclusions qualify the scope. Restricted-technology overseas deployment remains within proposed controls, with facilitation for own-cloud deployment. These provisions do not establish an already-effective blanket exemption for transferring weights or infrastructure know-how.[36](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/zcfb/dwmygl/art/2026/art_61446ab4beee4f63973d4d0ab3097648.html), [37](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/zwgk/zcjd/art/2026/art_b11d5c1e9374421e93e85dc87eba6e38.html) Materials controls affect semiconductor and industrial inputs through another channel. Suspensions leave licensing leverage intact and have different expiry dates.[38](https://policy.mofcom.gov.cn/claw/clawContent.shtml?id=102310)–[41](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/zfxxgk/fdzdgknr/ztfl/dwmygl/art/2024/art_8300c93ceef047debcf4da0ced080629.html)

Western equipment makers benefit from construction but face licensing cuts and domestic substitution. ASML's annual total-sales guide and quarterly system-sales share use different denominators; other makers report different periods. Exposure is not revenue certain to disappear.[45](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/937966/000162828026065813/a2026_10x14xpresentation.htm)–[49](https://www.tel.com/ir/irta3a00000006g5-att/fy27q1presentations-e.pdf)

**Taiwan and the bubble question.** TSMC acknowledges geographic concentration and interruption risks. Blockade, quarantine, shipping or energy disruption could stop deliveries without destroying fabs. Overseas expansion does not immediately recreate the manufacturing ecosystem.[42](https://investor.tsmc.com/sites/ir/sec-filings/2025_20F%20Report.pdf)

Taiwan's recent record equity close shows markets still capitalize substantial earnings.[43](https://www.brecorder.com/news/amp/40442826) Growth, discount rates, recovery assumptions and geopolitical risk are jointly priced. No reliable isolated TSMC discount, executable war-insurance quote or market-implied conflict probability was verified. Military counts measure activity, not probabilities.[44](https://www.mnd.gov.tw/en/News/PLAAct/88006)

China creates conditional pressure on Western inference returns, uneven commercial and subsidized overbuild exposure, and a Taiwan tail that can impair the entire stack despite healthy demand. Disclosed cloud profits, application losses and procurement timing support a differentiated capital-at-risk assessment; they do not establish broad inference-margin collapse or a China-wide AI bubble. Equipment suppliers can profit while customers earn inadequate returns. These conditional conclusions are graded C; utilization, incremental returns and security valuations remain inadequate to settle the bubble question.

## Conflicts and open questions

- **Review judgement — rents and bubble diagnosis:** the original note's Summary said China “competes away model rents” and treated inference-margin/overbuild risks as material (claim 40). The cross-check judges tariff samples insufficient to demonstrate realized rent destruction and puts more weight on profitable cloud services, application losses and procurement timing. Both views retain competition as a conditional risk; neither has matched-task switching, utilization or investment-cohort returns to establish a China-wide bubble.[26](https://www.alibabacloud.com/help/en/model-studio/model-pricing), [8](https://www.tencent.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Tencent-Announces-2026-Second-Quarter-Results.pdf), [51](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1577552/000110465926099220/tm2623667d1_ex99-1.htm)
- **Review judgement — financing and loss bearers:** the original note foregrounded negative FCF and equity funding and described lender losses conditionally. The cross-check assigns more weight to Tencent's announced notes and ByteDance's reported unsecured facility. Debt financing is now evidenced, but AI allocations, completed issuance/drawdown and ultimate losses remain unestablished.[52](https://www.tencent.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/E700_Pricing-Announcement.pdf), [53](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/bytedance-secures-296-billion-loan-in-ai-push-sources-say-4889439)
- **Review judgement — substitution constraints:** the original Summary treated software, packaging and yields as binding constraints and called Ascend a credible inference alternative. The cross-check views released Ascend kernels as stronger substitution evidence, while finding unquantified packaging/yield claims too categorical. The releases establish porting progress; manufacturing autonomy and comparable deployment economics remain open.[16](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20145), [18](https://www.huawei.com/en/news/2025/9/hc-xu-keynote-speech), [55](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/TileKernels), [56](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/FlashMLA/blob/main/docs/20260930-ascend-prefill-deep-dive.md)
- **ByteDance budget versus denial:** Reuters' anonymous sources put the 2025 budget above RMB150bn; ByteDance explicitly rejected the spending information. The original grade C is corrected to contested grade D. The separate May 2026 US$70bn scenario and US$50bn profit estimate remain C, reported, unverified; neither resolves the disputed earlier budget. The original Summary called ByteDance's prospective spending “much larger” than Alibaba's commitment; the cross-check judges that comparison unsafe because an annual scenario and a multiyear floor have different periods and potentially different scopes.[11](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/exclusivebytedance-plans-20-billion-capex-in-2025-mostly-on-ai-sources-say-3826261?ampMode=1), [12](https://theedgemalaysia.com/node/805120)
- **V4-Pro service-status conflict:** the September launch page planned rerouting V4-Pro requests after September 14, whereas the current official changelog says the API continues after that date. Huawei Cloud's September 22 retirement notice applies to its own MaaS service, not all hosts. Treat each notice's scope separately and verify the intended host's current service.[57](https://www.deepseek.com/en/news/deepseek-v4-1-flash/), [58](https://api-docs.deepseek.com/updates/), [59](https://www.huaweicloud.com/intl/en-us/notice/20260922173926811.html)
- **Efficiency versus scale:** modest disclosed training compute coexists with a reported giant serving build. The scopes differ. Needed: audited development spending and actual inference utilization.[13](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.19437v2), [14](https://media.springernature.com/original/springer-static/esm/art%3A10.1038%2Fs41586-025-09422-z/MediaObjects/41586_2025_9422_MOESM1_ESM.pdf), [17](https://www.bloomberglinea.com/negocios/deepseek-planea-instalar-160000-chips-huawei-en-un-megacentro-para-reducir-su-dependencia-de-nvidia/)
- **Substitution versus scarcity:** Ascend post-training, released kernels and domestic memory progress support substitution; reported HBM scarcity and incomplete original-pretraining disclosure limit the autonomy claim.[55](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/TileKernels), [56](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/FlashMLA/blob/main/docs/20260930-ascend-prefill-deep-dive.md) Needed: accepted shipments, good-die yields and repeatable workload economics.[16](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20145)–[24](https://www.ymtc.com/en/technicalintroduction.html)
- **Commoditization versus monetization:** cheap tariffs compete with premium tiers and improving cloud pricing. Needed: matched-task customer switching and contribution margins, not token prices alone.[8](https://www.tencent.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Tencent-Announces-2026-Second-Quarter-Results.pdf), [26](https://www.alibabacloud.com/help/en/model-studio/model-pricing)–[29](https://www.minimax.io/blog/minimax-m25)
- **Boom versus bubble:** cash commitments, new equity and prospective debt increase capital at risk; disclosed cloud profit and the prepayment-adjusted FCF counterfactual are contrary evidence to a blanket loss-making-operations diagnosis.[51](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1577552/000110465926099220/tm2623667d1_ex99-1.htm), [8](https://www.tencent.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Tencent-Announces-2026-Second-Quarter-Results.pdf), [52](https://www.tencent.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/E700_Pricing-Announcement.pdf) Needed: utilization, lease commitments, depreciation and returns by investment cohort.[51](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1577552/000110465926099220/tm2623667d1_ex99-1.htm), [8](https://www.tencent.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Tencent-Announces-2026-Second-Quarter-Results.pdf), [12](https://theedgemalaysia.com/node/805120)
- **Policy closure versus continued exports:** temporary registration is effective; the draft final regime adds oversight while preserving explicit exclusions and controls on restricted-technology overseas deployment. Implementation could diverge; the final catalogue, licences and treatment of future releases remain unresolved.[36](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/zcfb/dwmygl/art/2026/art_61446ab4beee4f63973d4d0ab3097648.html), [37](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/zwgk/zcjd/art/2026/art_b11d5c1e9374421e93e85dc87eba6e38.html)
- **Unverified claims:** revised Alibaba total; Tencent/Baidu full-year capex guidance; audited ByteDance spending and loan drawdown; Tencent note issuance; DeepSeek round closing and site financing/delivery; advanced-node yields, HBM shipments, all-in R1 budget and Taiwan implied probability.
