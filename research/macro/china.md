# China: supply competitor, demand pool and policy risk

*Last updated: 2026-10-17 · Status: draft · Author: Codex/OpenAI · Reviewer: none*

## Summary

- China expands infrastructure demand, competes away model rents and creates geopolitical supply risk. Equipment sales can rise while inference returns weaken.
- Established internet businesses are profitable, but recent infrastructure spending exceeds operating cash generation. Alibaba also raised equity; private-lab financing remains opaque.
- Alibaba's RMB380bn commitment covers three years, with a subsequent announced increase but no verified replacement total. ByteDance's much larger prospective spending remains reported, unverified.
- DeepSeek's V3 compute estimate excludes prior research; its later R1 disclosure covers specified post-training, excluding inherited base training and broader development.
- Ascend is a credible domestic alternative, particularly for inference. HBM, packaging, software and yields constrain delivery and economics.
- Open weights and cheap APIs undermine scarcity pricing; adequate provider returns and universal workload substitution remain unproven.
- Taiwan disruption threatens the whole stack. Equity prices and military activity cannot establish a numerical invasion probability.

## Analysis

**Demand and financing.** China expands infrastructure demand, but group capex includes general cloud, CPUs and facilities. Alibaba's fiscal year ends in March; Tencent and Baidu use calendar years. Private-company budgets are prospective. Adding these figures into “China AI capex” would manufacture comparability.

Alibaba's multi-year commitment is a floor, with an announced but unquantified increase. Actual spending and its capacity target support acceleration. Component inflation and procurement timing mean expenditure need not translate proportionately into useful compute. Revenue growth is encouraging; negative FCF and the equity placement show shareholders funding expansion and bearing dilution and impairment risk.[1](https://www.alibabagroup.com/en-US/document-1830678592242057216)–[5](https://www.alibabagroup.com/en-US/document-2029365886510432256), [50](https://home.alibabagroup.com/en-US/document-2041268239081668608)

Tencent remains profitable, while cash capex exceeds operating cash generation. Management attributes some cash pressure to compute prepayments and describes a more favourable cloud pricing environment: evidence against a universal price war. Baidu similarly invests beyond current operating cash; its investment holdings provide liquidity, although “cash and investments” exceeds immediately available cash.[6](https://static.www.tencent.com/uploads/2026/04/09/62d786fcf3d3c8cb7e54791ee95439ac.pdf)–[10](https://ir.baidu.com/news-releases/news-release-details/baidu-announces-second-quarter-2026-results)

ByteDance's reported escalation differs in date and potentially scope from its earlier budget. Reported profit suggests funding capacity, but no complete project financing, debt or lease schedule was verified.[11](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/exclusivebytedance-plans-20-billion-capex-in-2025-mostly-on-ai-sources-say-3826261?ampMode=1), [12](https://theedgemalaysia.com/node/805120) Private labs depend on investors and infrastructure partners; government funding also supports semiconductor manufacturers.[25](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/chinas-memory-chip-makers-ride-ai-boom-to-new-power--and-us-scrutiny-4810472) Disappointing returns first reach shareholders, strategic backers and asset owners. Lenders, landlords and public funders bear losses where contracts or guarantees create exposure; those arrangements remain incompletely disclosed.

**DeepSeek and domestic supply.** V3's arithmetic measures an official run at assumed rental prices, excluding earlier experiments. It is neither hardware acquisition cost nor the cost of discovering the recipe.[13](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.19437v2) The later Nature supplement separately estimates specified R1 stages on top of an inherited base. Neither figure establishes a complete development budget or serving margin.[14](https://media.springernature.com/original/springer-static/esm/art%3A10.1038%2Fs41586-025-09422-z/MediaObjects/41586_2025_9422_MOESM1_ESM.pdf)

V4 is beyond reported development: the preview exists, and a separate team's primary paper demonstrates V4-family post-training on Ascend.[15](https://deepseek.com/en/news/v4-preview/), [16](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20145) This does not prove original frontier pretraining ran entirely on Huawei. Bloomberg's later site report explicitly concerns inference; the proposed accelerators fill only part of the site, without establishing installed capacity or utilization.[17](https://www.bloomberglinea.com/negocios/deepseek-planea-instalar-160000-chips-huawei-en-un-megacentro-para-reducir-su-dependencia-de-nvidia/)

Huawei's roadmap cannot settle manufacturing yield, qualified HBM supply or cluster economics.[18](https://www.huawei.com/en/news/2025/9/hc-xu-keynote-speech) Component-chip production estimates are not finished-accelerator counts. HBM controls and reported scarcity-driven processor price increases contradict unlimited cheap domestic compute.[17](https://www.bloomberglinea.com/negocios/deepseek-planea-instalar-160000-chips-huawei-en-un-megacentro-para-reducir-su-dependencia-de-nvidia/)–[20](https://www.bis.gov/press-release/commerce-strengthens-export-controls-restrict-chinas-capability-produce-advanced-semiconductors-military)

SMIC's utilization and positive gross margin show manufacturing demand, without disclosing advanced-node good-die output or Ascend profitability. Original teardown evidence supports progress while contradicting leading-node parity.[21](https://smic.cdn.shwebspace.com/uploads/6a7d7c4f/ER_EN.pdf), [22](https://www.techinsights.com/ja/node/60833) CXMT DRAM and YMTC NAND widen domestic options; NAND cannot substitute for accelerator HBM, and a DRAM process announcement does not establish packaging yield or HBM qualification.[23](https://www.cxmt.com/news/info_97.html), [24](https://www.ymtc.com/en/technicalintroduction.html) Substitution can reduce import dependence while increasing resource cost per useful token.

**Prices and inference margins.** Open weights enable competing hosts and credible migration threats; cheap APIs weaken scarcity pricing. The tariff samples establish offers, not a quality-adjusted price series or a causal estimate of global deflation. Not every hosted variant in an open-weight family has released weights.[26](https://www.alibabacloud.com/help/en/model-studio/model-pricing)–[29](https://www.minimax.io/blog/minimax-m25)

Western margins collapse only if substitution works at comparable task success, latency, reliability and governance, and price reductions outrun cost reductions and usage growth. Cheaper tokens can expand agent usage and equipment demand while destroying model rents. Kimi's premium tier and Tencent's pricing commentary contradict universal commoditization.[8](https://www.tencent.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Tencent-Announces-2026-Second-Quarter-Results.pdf), [27](https://platform.kimi.com/docs/pricing/chat) Provider margins after depreciation, power and unsuccessful outputs remain unknown.

**Permission from both governments.** H20 illustrates stranded inventory risk. A disclosed revenue-share expectation does not establish a statutory tax or collections. H200 subsequently operates under a distinct combination of conditional US licensing, Chinese restrictions and a separate tariff.[30](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581025000209/nvda-20250727.htm)–[33](https://media.bis.gov/press-release/department-commerce-revises-license-review-policy-semiconductors-exported-china) Blackwell variants and tracking proposals do not establish a broad approved data-centre channel.[34](https://www.tradingview.com/news/reuters.com%2C2025%3Anewsml_L2N3V202J%3A0-nvidia-s-new-rtx6000d-chip-for-china-finds-little-favour-with-major-firms-sources-say/), [35](https://www.congress.gov/bill/119th-congress/house-bill/3447)

MOFCOM combines a draft catalogue revision with already-effective registration. Its public-domain and service exclusions contradict a blanket model-export ban.[36](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/zcfb/dwmygl/art/2026/art_61446ab4beee4f63973d4d0ab3097648.html), [37](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/zwgk/zcjd/art/2026/art_b11d5c1e9374421e93e85dc87eba6e38.html) Materials controls affect semiconductor and industrial inputs through another channel. Suspensions leave licensing leverage intact and have different expiry dates.[38](https://policy.mofcom.gov.cn/claw/clawContent.shtml?id=102310)–[41](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/zfxxgk/fdzdgknr/ztfl/dwmygl/art/2024/art_8300c93ceef047debcf4da0ced080629.html)

Western equipment makers benefit from construction but face licensing cuts and domestic substitution. ASML's annual total-sales guide and quarterly system-sales share use different denominators; other makers report different periods. Exposure is not revenue certain to disappear.[45](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/937966/000162828026065813/a2026_10x14xpresentation.htm)–[49](https://www.tel.com/ir/irta3a00000006g5-att/fy27q1presentations-e.pdf)

**Taiwan and the bubble question.** TSMC acknowledges geographic concentration and interruption risks. Blockade, quarantine, shipping or energy disruption could stop deliveries without destroying fabs. Overseas expansion does not immediately recreate the manufacturing ecosystem.[42](https://investor.tsmc.com/sites/ir/sec-filings/2025_20F%20Report.pdf)

Taiwan's recent record equity close shows markets still capitalize substantial earnings.[43](https://www.brecorder.com/news/amp/40442826) Growth, discount rates, recovery assumptions and geopolitical risk are jointly priced. No reliable isolated TSMC discount, executable war-insurance quote or market-implied conflict probability was verified. Military counts measure activity, not probabilities.[44](https://www.mnd.gov.tw/en/News/PLAAct/88006)

China thus threatens Western inference returns through competition, adds a parallel commercial and subsidized overbuild risk, and creates a Taiwan tail that can impair the entire stack despite healthy demand. Equipment suppliers can profit while customers earn inadequate returns. These conditional conclusions are graded C, without assigning an unsupported conflict probability.

## Conflicts and open questions

- **Efficiency versus scale:** modest disclosed training compute coexists with a reported giant serving build. The scopes differ. Needed: audited development spending and actual inference utilization.[13](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.19437v2), [14](https://media.springernature.com/original/springer-static/esm/art%3A10.1038%2Fs41586-025-09422-z/MediaObjects/41586_2025_9422_MOESM1_ESM.pdf), [17](https://www.bloomberglinea.com/negocios/deepseek-planea-instalar-160000-chips-huawei-en-un-megacentro-para-reducir-su-dependencia-de-nvidia/)
- **Substitution versus scarcity:** Ascend post-training and domestic memory progress support autonomy; HBM scarcity and incomplete original-pretraining disclosure limit the claim. Needed: accepted shipments, good-die yields and repeatable workload economics.[16](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20145)–[24](https://www.ymtc.com/en/technicalintroduction.html)
- **Commoditization versus monetization:** cheap tariffs compete with premium tiers and improving cloud pricing. Needed: matched-task customer switching and contribution margins, not token prices alone.[8](https://www.tencent.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Tencent-Announces-2026-Second-Quarter-Results.pdf), [26](https://www.alibabacloud.com/help/en/model-studio/model-pricing)–[29](https://www.minimax.io/blog/minimax-m25)
- **Boom versus bubble:** cash burn and new equity are warning signs; strong existing businesses and growing demand are the strongest contrary evidence. Needed: utilization, lease commitments, depreciation and returns by investment cohort.[3](https://data.alibabagroup.com/ecms-files/1532295521/fa5d65fc-9b3e-4e82-a8fc-4ce1c3e2c407/Alibaba%20Group%20Announces%20June%20Quarter%202026%20Results.pdf)–[12](https://theedgemalaysia.com/node/805120)
- **Policy closure versus continued exports:** the draft adds oversight while preserving explicit exclusions. Implementation could diverge; the final catalogue, licences and treatment of future releases remain unresolved.[36](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/zcfb/dwmygl/art/2026/art_61446ab4beee4f63973d4d0ab3097648.html), [37](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/zwgk/zcjd/art/2026/art_b11d5c1e9374421e93e85dc87eba6e38.html)
