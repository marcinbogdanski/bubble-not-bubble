# Power and datacenter real estate

*Last updated: 2026-10-17 · Status: draft · Author: Codex / GPT-6 · Reviewer: none*

## Summary

- Power, cooling and buildings form a separate capital layer. Regional energization constrains deployment; chips also remain constrained.
- Forecasts remain wide. Queues include speculative applications; announced capacity is not consumption or commissioned IT load.
- Equipment incumbents and established colocation businesses are profitable. Their margins do not establish that every financed campus earns its cost of capital.
- Hyperscalers, utilities, REITs, funds and bondholders share exposure; guarantees can transfer risk back to tenants.
- Selected campus budgets total $82bn of commitments. A consolidated capital stock remains unverified.
- Unpowered shells, merchant developments and conditional tenant obligations are vulnerable. A demand shortfall would initially reduce new starts, then threaten refinancing, lease collection and residual values.
- Biggest uncertainty: conversion of signed capacity into paying, energized demand.

## Analysis

Deliverable electricity, permits, cooling and a creditworthy customer make a shell useful. Annual TWh, peak-load MW, IT nameplate and redundant generator ratings differ. The [pipeline CSV](/data/datacenter-pipeline.csv) preserves these distinctions; do not sum it as operating supply.

LBNL models server deployment; EPRI's updated scenarios imply substantially more US load, while IEA's global central trajectory barely changes. EIA forecasts commercial-sector growth, not separately measured AI demand. None is an order book. PJM reduced near-term demand after better vetting; queues require probability weights. Its generation queue differs from ERCOT's load queue: processing a generator does not commission it; registering load does not establish demand. Stage construction and chip delivery against energization milestones: early purchases expose chips to financing and obsolescence before revenue; waiting risks supply slots. Hut's structures show how highly leveraged construction can obtain investment-grade project financing, with non-recourse protecting the parent subject to contractual exceptions. Excalibur's reported tenant and falling bond price do not establish its full guarantee package. Bond discounts reprice execution, duration, liquidity and credit risk; they are not defaults. [S53](https://about.fb.com/news/2025/10/meta-blue-owl-capital-develop-hyperion-data-center/) [S54](https://ca.finance.yahoo.com/news/investors-dump-data-centre-bonds-132608292.html) [S55](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1964789/000110465926090025/hut-20260630x10q.htm) [S56](https://www.hut8.com/news-insights/press-releases/hut-8-reports-first-quarter-2026-results)

Consumer politics can change project economics. NJBPU identifies datacenter demand among the causes of higher bills; EIA's national price forecast cannot isolate its causal contribution. Conversely, Entergy says Meta covers incremental infrastructure and contributes savings, and Oracle says Lighthouse costs are ring-fenced. Verify these protections against billing and collateral requirements. Texas's later environmental-permit pause weakens the assumption that onsite gas universally escapes political constraints. [S2](https://www.eia.gov/outlooks/steo/archives/oct26.pdf) [S43](https://www.nj.gov/bpu/newsroom/2025/approved/20250212.html) [S58](https://www.entergy.com/news/entergy-louisiana-announces-a-new-agreement-with-meta-that-will-deliver-an-additional-2b-in-customer-savings) [S62](https://www.oracle.com/news/announcement/blog/a-responsible-path-forward-for-project-lighthouse-2026-09-16/) [S42](https://gov.texas.gov/news/post/governor-abbott-directs-tceq-to-halt-data-center-permits)

In a shortfall, unused land and uncommitted phases absorb losses first through write-downs and cancelled starts. Empty shells still incur interest, taxes and maintenance; specialized cooling may need costly adaptation. Enforceable leases initially shift underutilization losses to tenants, but defaults, negotiated concessions and renewal decisions transmit them back to owners and lenders. Guarantees delay transmission without creating demand. Site-specific turbines and gas connections can strand capital, although resalable turbines or grid-connected generation retain alternative uses. Suppliers face weaker orders and factory utilization after cancellation protection runs out. Utility losses depend on prudence findings, customer security and rate allocation. These mechanisms do not establish existing impairments.
