## Summary

- PJM's July 14, 2026 auction fell 6,831 MW short of the 2028/29 reliability requirement at the price cap, while retaining a 14.7% reserve margin; only 525 MW of new generation/uprates cleared. This is an RTO-wide adequacy gap, not a campus-connection measure. TSMC still reported demand above chip supply on October 15, 2026. [S9](https://www.pjm.com/-/media/DotCom/about-pjm/newsroom/2026-releases/20260714-pjm-capacity-auction-procures-138318-mw-of-generation-resources.pdf) [S59](https://investor.tsmc.com/english/encrypt/files/encrypt_file/reports/2026-10/eba4fac72873bf546b669bd2b5d973df5c93215d/TSMC%203Q26%20Transcript.pdf)
- EPRI's February 2026 scenarios put US datacenter electricity use at 9–17% of the national total by 2030. These conditional projections, connection requests and announced campus MW are not measured consumption. [S64](https://restservice.epri.com/publicattachment/96784)
- In Q2 2026, Vertiv reported a 19.5% operating margin, GE Vernova's Power segment an 18.8% EBITDA margin (group net margin 5.8%) with $5.1bn of group free cash flow, and Equinix $665m of operating income. Removing the rise in non-recurring revenue at an assumed 100% margin leaves Equinix's adjusted EBITDA margin at 50.6% (B diagnostic, not disclosed ex-fee results). Incumbent profits do not establish returns on unfinished campuses. - Nvidia's Q2 FY2027 filing discloses August 2026 guarantees capped at $105bn for 4.25 GW of OpenAI-related leases in Ohio, plus an option for another 3.8 GW of credit support. At July 26 it separately lists $25bn of uncommenced datacenter leases and $20bn of third-party leases it expects to reassign; reassignment delays can leave Nvidia bearing rent. These are 2025 announcements, not current budgets or aggregate funded/deployed AI capital. A demand shortfall can appear in financing prices, land values, cancelled starts or tenant concessions; the order depends on each project's funding and lease terms. Empty shells still incur interest, taxes and maintenance; specialized cooling may need costly adaptation. Enforceable leases initially shift underutilization losses to tenants, but defaults, negotiated concessions and renewal decisions transmit them back to owners and lenders. Guarantees delay transmission without creating demand. Site-specific turbines and gas connections can strand capital, although resalable turbines or grid-connected generation retain alternative uses. Suppliers face weaker orders and factory utilization after cancellation protection runs out. Utility losses depend on prudence findings, customer security and rate allocation. These mechanisms do not establish existing impairments.

The [power-layer forecast](../../forecasts/register.md) credit branch overlaps the Oracle row through Oracle-tenanted project loans such as Jupiter, and the neocloud row through CoreWeave-tenanted landlords such as Applied Digital. The layer forecasts are not independent loss events; holding the existing probabilities does not justify adding them.
