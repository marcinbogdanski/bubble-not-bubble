# Forecast register

*Status: skeleton. Questions are drafted; probabilities will be assigned once
the research notes are complete and will carry a date and reasoning. Rules:
see [Methodology](/report/methodology). Horizons: 6, 12 and 24 months from
2026-10-17, i.e. 2027-04-17, 2027-10-17 and 2028-10-17.*

## How to read this

Each forecast is a yes/no question with a resolution source. The probability
is the lead agent's considered estimate after reading the evidence and the
reviews. Each revision is appended to the forecast's history with its cause.
Per-layer "impairment" summaries roll up the forecasts that bear on that
layer.

## Candidate questions (to be finalised)

### Demand and revenue

- **D1.** Will OpenAI publicly report, or be credibly reported (Reuters, The
  Information, FT, WSJ), to have annualised revenue below $X at any point
  before the horizon date? (threshold to be set from the research note)
- **D2.** Will the combined reported annualised revenue of OpenAI and
  Anthropic exceed $Y by the horizon date?
- **D3.** Will Microsoft, Alphabet or Amazon disclose a sequential decline in
  cloud revenue growth of more than Z points in any quarter before the
  horizon?

### Capex and supply

- **C1.** Will aggregate 2027 capex guidance from Microsoft, Alphabet,
  Amazon and Meta (as first given in their Q4 2026 reports) be below their
  2026 actual capex?
- **C2.** Will any of the four cut its current-year capex guidance by more
  than 10% in any quarterly report before the horizon?
- **C3.** Will Nvidia report a year-on-year decline in data-center revenue in
  any quarter reported before the horizon?
- **C4.** Will TSMC report a year-on-year decline in monthly revenue for
  three consecutive months before the horizon?

### Pricing

- **P1.** Will a published H100 rental index fall below $1.50 per GPU-hour
  (or a threshold set from the pricing note) for three consecutive months?
- **P2.** Will contract DRAM prices fall more than 30% from their 2026 peak?

### Credit and financing

- **F1.** Will CoreWeave default, restructure its debt, or be acquired at a
  price below its IPO price before the horizon?
- **F2.** Will Oracle's senior unsecured credit rating be cut to
  non-investment grade by any major agency before the horizon?
- **F3.** Will OpenAI publicly cancel, defer or reduce any announced compute
  commitment of $10B or more?
- **F4.** Will any AI-infrastructure SPV or GPU-backed loan of $1B or more
  enter default or forced restructuring?
- **F5.** Will a frontier lab (OpenAI, Anthropic, xAI, Mistral, SSI,
  Thinking Machines) raise a priced round at a lower valuation than its
  previous one?

### Markets

- **M1.** Will an equal-weighted basket of NVDA, AVGO, TSM, ORCL, CRWV, AMD,
  MU, VRT draw down 40% or more from its 2026 peak on a closing basis?
- **M2.** Will Nvidia's market capitalisation fall below $2 trillion?
- **M3.** Will the Nasdaq-100 draw down 30% or more from its peak?
